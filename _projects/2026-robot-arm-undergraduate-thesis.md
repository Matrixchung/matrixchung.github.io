description: >-
  Designed and built a 6-DoF robotic arm integrating custom FOC joint drives, dual absolute encoders, a CAN-FD bus and ROS 2 control stack. Through proper sim-to-real modeling, precise force feedback and gravity compensation are realized. The experiments show that the system achieved a payload above 2.5 kg, no-load repeatability within ±0.012 mm, and visual tracking at over 30 Hz. This project was recognized as Outstanding Undergraduate Graduation Designs of UESTC.
