  A gripper designed for Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI) style embodied data collection, suitable for both handheld operation and mounting on the robot arm. It extends the UMI concept with extra tactile sensing and current-based contact force control. An RGB-D stereo camera tracks the gripper pose through SLAM-based odometry, and the tactile sensing module is based on a modified FlexiTac Mini design with a smaller but higher-performance readout board, achieving a tactile streaming rate above 60 FPS.
